About

WeSave checks Indian landfills for fire every 3 hours, using NASA satellite data.

  1. Detect. Fixed rules decide if a satellite hotspot is a possible fire.
  2. Smoke area. Wind direction shows where smoke may go, and which schools and hospitals are inside.
  3. Write. AI writes the alert from those facts. 11 safety checks review it.
  4. Send. Nothing is sent until a person approves.

Running on AWS: EventBridge · Lambda · DynamoDB · S3 · SNS (ap-south-1) · Bedrock via Strands (us-east-1)

Limits

  • Detects surface fires only. Fires that smoulder underground can be missed.
  • Pixel size is about 375 m (VIIRS) to 1 km or more (MODIS). A hotspot can come from a nearby heat source.
  • Satellites pass a few times a day. Cloud and thick smoke can block detection.
  • A detection is not a confirmation. A person must check.
  • The smoke zone is a screening sector from wind direction. It is not a pollution forecast.
